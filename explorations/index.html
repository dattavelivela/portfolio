Portfolio explorations

Seven directions, all built from the same real content and screenshots — working prototypes, not mockups. 01–05 are ways of indexing work. 06–07 added depth and a thesis. 08 found a voice. 09 is the craft pass — the first one whose design comes from the subject matter rather than from a template.

What the benchmarks reward, in their own words: measurable outcomes · storytelling that ties user problems to design decisions · side experiments showing curiosity · a bold visual identity. 06 and 07 are aimed squarely at those.

The current site is untouched at ../index.html.

01

The 40-second scan

Dense two-column CV layout with a sticky rail and scrollspy. A recruiter mode toggle strips images and prose away, leaving only outcomes — the fastest possible read of the same page.

09

Ledger of work — the craft pass

Built from the subject itself. Procurement's native form is the ruled document, so the portfolio is a ledger: work as numbered line items, outcomes sitting in the column where amounts belong, a carried-forward total. Greenbar accounting paper and stamp violet — no cream, no terracotta. Zodiak serif with tabular figures. Opens with native <details>, so it needs zero JavaScript.
